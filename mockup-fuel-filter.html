Trade-offs

  • A (Green border) — Best balance: clear indication without being overwhelming. Works well in both light/dark. Maintains pill-in-pill aesthetic.
  • B (Color fill) — Most obvious, but changes the entire look of the pill. Can feel aggressive when multiple types are selected. White text on green may reduce readability for longer labels.
  • C (Dot badge) — Most subtle. Might still feel too similar to inactive state. The green dot adds minimal visual weight.
